AQUADECK / README.TXT

Light from the film.
Not an animation on top.

Moving video frames are sampled into the translucent control lenses, not washed across the whole chassis. Lens sampling bends that image: WebGL where available, a simpler Canvas 2D lens otherwise. The interface stays ordinary, keyboard-accessible HTML.

Renderer, motion & browser notes +

Keyboard: Tab and Shift+Tab move through controls. Enter or Space activates buttons. On the timeline, arrows seek 5 seconds, Page Up/Down seek 10 seconds, and Home/End jump to either end. Volume uses native arrows and Home/End. Escape exits fullscreen.

Effects switch off by default with reduced motion. Video never autoplays. There is no HDR or Apple Liquid Glass claim here. All color is standard dynamic range.

Fullscreen uses the browser API where available; iPhone may show its native video controls. The volume slider follows browser support. The original motion study includes a quiet synthetic audio bed and descriptive captions.
