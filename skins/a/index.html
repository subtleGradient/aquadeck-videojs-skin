ELECTRIC AQUA / LIQUID CHROME

Leave the rectangle behind.

Polished fins. Glassy blue controls. A video window suspended in liquid metal. LIQUID ORBIT brings the wild optimism of turn-of-the-millennium media players to an everyday, keyboard-accessible Video.js player.

Tab moves through controls. Space or Enter activates buttons. Timeline arrows seek; Home and End jump to either end. Escape leaves fullscreen. On iPhone, use the device buttons for volume.
